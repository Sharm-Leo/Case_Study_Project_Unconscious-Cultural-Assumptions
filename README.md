# Beyond Time Zones: How Unconscious Cultural Assumptions Impact Remote Team Success  
### SPG Project 2026 — Team 43

This repository contains the complete documentation, research process, and deliverables for the SPG Project 2026 conducted by **Team 43**. The project explored how unconscious cultural assumptions influence communication, collaboration, trust, and overall success in remote teams operating across different time zones and cultural backgrounds.

As the **Team Leader**, I coordinated both phases of the project — from team formation and planning to conducting research, interviews, analysis, and final presentation delivery.

---

# Project Overview

Remote work has transformed global collaboration, but cultural misunderstandings and hidden assumptions continue to create challenges within distributed teams. This project investigates:

- How unconscious cultural biases affect remote teamwork
- Communication barriers across cultures and time zones
- Differences in work expectations, leadership styles, and collaboration habits
- Strategies for improving inclusivity and effectiveness in remote teams

The research combined academic study, qualitative interviews, team observations, and collaborative analysis to develop practical insights into cross-cultural remote team dynamics.
